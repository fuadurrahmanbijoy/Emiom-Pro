Printer-shop workflow
1. Print SIDE 1 on all A4 sheets.
2. Collect the complete stack.
3. Re-feed the stack according to the selected setting.
4. Print SIDE 2 on the same sheets.
5. Fold each sheet through the middle, stack them, and staple along the fold.
