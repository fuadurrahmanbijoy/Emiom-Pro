# Visual Guide: A Victorian Academic Book

*Reference for layout, typography, and print. Model: the page of Darwin's* On the Origin of Species *(1859). Audience: first-year university students. Every decision below comes from your answers.*

---

## 1. The Look in One Paragraph

A dense, dignified, Victorian-style academic book on A4, printed in near-black ink on warm cream uncoated paper, bound in burgundy cloth with gold lettering. Pages feel packed with knowledge: justified text with hyphenation, a narrow side column of notes, footnotes, running headers, and small flourishes between sections. No colour anywhere inside. Hierarchy is carried by weight, italics, small capitals, and rules.

---

## 2. Page Format

| Item | Specification |
| --- | --- |
| Trim size | A4 (210 × 297 mm) portrait |
| Bleed | 3 mm (needed only for cover and chapter-opener art that touches the edge) |
| Margins | Top 20 mm, bottom 22 mm, inner (gutter) 25 mm, outer 15 mm |
| Main text column | About 120 mm wide |
| Gap | 8 mm |
| Side column | About 42 mm wide, on the **outer** side (right on right-hand pages, left on left-hand pages) |
| Lines per page | About 50 |
| Chapter start | Always on a right-hand page; leave the left page blank if needed |

**Why this works:** A4 is wide, so full-width text would run past 100 characters per line and tire the eye. The side column uses the extra width for marginal notes and keeps the main line at about 65-70 characters.

---

## 3. Typefaces

All free and open-licence (Google Fonts), so you can embed them in print files without cost.

| Role | Typeface | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Body text | **Libre Caslon Text** | Sturdy, slightly full, very readable; closest free match to "old but readable." Fallback: EB Garamond (lighter, more delicate) |
| Headings | Libre Caslon Text **Bold**, capitals, letterspaced | Gives a full-bodied Victorian feel |
| Chapter titles | **IM Fell English** (SC for small capitals) | Authentic old-press character for the mood-setting display lines |
| Mood line and description | IM Fell English *Italic* |  |
| Side notes and footnotes | Libre Caslon Text, smaller |  |
| Cover title | Libre Caslon Display or IM Fell English, capitals, widely spaced | Gold foil |

**Rule:** Use only these families. Variety comes from weight, italic, and small capitals, never from extra fonts.

---

## 4. Text Setting

| Element | Specification |
| --- | --- |
| Body size | 10.5 pt on 14 pt leading |
| Alignment | Justified |
| Hyphenation | On. At least 3 letters before and after the break; no more than 2 hyphens in a row |
| Paragraphs | First-line indent of about 4.5 mm, **no space between paragraphs** |
| After a heading | First paragraph has no indent |
| Widows and orphans | Never fewer than 2 lines at the top or bottom of a page |
| Word spacing | Tight and even; avoid rivers of white space (adjust hyphenation, not letter-spacing) |
| Quotations | Longer quotes indented on both sides, 9.5 pt, no quotation marks |

---

## 5. Headings (Hierarchy by Weight, No Colour)

1. **Chapter title.** Full-page opener (see section 6).
2. **Section heading (level 1).** Bold capitals, 11 pt, letterspaced, centred, with a thin rule above and below. About 14 pt of space before, 8 pt after.
3. **Subsection heading (level 2).** Bold small capitals, left-aligned, on its own line.
4. **Minor heading (level 3).** *Italic*, regular weight, on its own line.
5. **Run-in heading (level 4, optional).** Small capitals at the start of the paragraph, followed by a full stop.

Sections are numbered in the contents only if you wish ("Section 1.2"). Do not number more than two levels.

---

## 6. The Chapter Opener (Full Page)

From top to bottom on one page:

1. Thin double-line frame around an **engraved illustration**, occupying roughly the upper half. Choose classic public-domain plates (Biodiversity Heritage Library, Internet Archive book images, Wikimedia Commons).
2. Small capitals, widely spaced: **CHAPTER THE FIRST**
3. **Chapter title** in IM Fell English, about 28 pt, centred.
4. A **mood line** in italic beneath it (one short phrase that sets the tone, for example a question or an image).
5. A small ornament or thin rule.
6. A **brief description** of the chapter: 3-4 lines, centred, in 10 pt italic, narrow measure.
7. Page number small at the foot. No running header on this page.

The first page of text after the opener begins with a **drop cap** (3 lines deep) in Libre Caslon Text bold. Use drop caps at chapter starts only.

---

## 7. Page Furniture

- **Running headers.** Left-hand page: the book title in small capitals. Right-hand page: the chapter title in small capitals. A thin rule sits below, and the page number is at the outer top corner.
- **Page numbers.** On every page. Chapter openers carry the number small at the bottom centre. Front matter uses lowercase Roman numerals (i, ii, iii); the main book uses Arabic numerals starting at 1.
- **Flourishes.** A centred ornament (❦ or ⁂) between major sections; a short centred thin rule for minor breaks. Keep to one or two ornament styles throughout.
- **Footnotes.** 8.5 pt on 10.5 pt leading, set at the foot of the page beneath a short thin rule (about 25 mm), numbered in order within each chapter.
- **Marginal notes (side column).** Two kinds, always distinguishable by style:
  - *Paragraph summaries:* short, one or two lines, in *italic* 8.5 pt.
  - **Key-term notes:** the term in ***bold italic***, followed by a one-line plain definition in roman type.

Side notes align with the top line of the paragraph they belong to. If two notes collide, the second moves down; never cut a note short.

---

## 8. Marking Key Terms (for Beginners)

1. **First appearance in the text:** ***bold italic***.
2. **Side column:** a brief key-term note with a plain one-line definition.
3. **Back of the book:** a fuller glossary with longer definitions and the chapter where the term first appears.
4. After the first appearance, the term is set in ordinary type.
5. Italics alone are used for emphasis, foreign words, and titles of works; bold italic is reserved for key terms so the two never get confused.

---

## 9. Figures, Tables, and Key-Term Boxes

Restrained and used only where they help.

- **Figures.** Line-engraving style drawings or simple line diagrams, black only. Caption beneath in 9 pt italic, preceded by **Fig. 1.** in small capitals. Number figures continuously through the book, or by chapter (Fig. 3.2), but keep one system.
- **Tables.** Horizontal rules only: one above, one below, and one under the column headings. No vertical lines, no shading. Caption above in small capitals.
- **Definition boxes.** Thin double-line border, no fill, a small-capitals label (DEFINITION or IN SHORT) at the top. Use sparingly, at most once or twice per chapter.
- **Wide figures.** May span the main column and side column together; never overlap the margin.

---

## 10. Front and Back Matter

**Front matter:** half-title, title page, copyright page, (optional dedication), contents, a short **"To the Reader"** note explaining how to read the side column, footnotes, and bold-italic terms, then preface.

**Contents page:** chapter titles in small capitals, dot leaders to page numbers, chapter numbers in Roman numerals.

**Back matter:** glossary, further reading, notes on sources, index.

---

## 11. Colour, Paper, and Ink

| Item | Specification |
| --- | --- |
| Ink | 100% black (K only) for all text and line art. No colour inside the book |
| Paper | Natural cream or ivory **uncoated** stock, about 90-100 gsm, warm (sometimes called "cream," "vanilla," or "book wove"). Avoid bright white and avoid glossy |
| Aged look | Comes from the paper stock itself, with clean pages. No printed stains or textures |
| Opacity | Choose a paper with good opacity so text does not show through on dense pages |

Always order a **sample proof** on your chosen stock before the full print run.

---

## 12. The Cover

- **Binding:** hardback, case-bound, with sewn (not glued) signatures so a thick A4 book opens flat and lasts.
- **Cloth:** burgundy book cloth, textured.
- **Front:** title in **gold foil**, capitals, widely spaced in a classic serif; a thin double-line border stamped in gold or blind-embossed; author name smaller beneath.
- **Spine:** title and author in gold, with thin gold rules near the top and bottom.
- **Extras (optional):** cream or gold headbands, a burgundy or gold ribbon marker, plain cream or marbled endpapers.

---

## 13. Production Checklist

1. Build the master pages first: margins, side column, running headers, page numbers.
2. Set up paragraph styles for each element above before writing pages.
3. Use a layout tool that supports side columns and footnotes: Affinity Publisher or Adobe InDesign (visual), or LaTeX with a sidenotes package (free, precise, steeper to learn).
4. Export a print PDF (PDF/X) with fonts embedded and bleed included.
5. Check for widows, orphans, bad hyphenation, and side-note collisions on every page.
6. Print a proof copy on the intended paper before approving the run.

---

## 14. Open Choices Still to Confirm

- Libre Caslon Text versus EB Garamond for the body (Caslon is fuller; Garamond is finer).
- Chapter art: one illustration per chapter, or a repeating decorative border with changing art.
- Whether figures are numbered by chapter (3.2) or continuously (Fig. 14).
